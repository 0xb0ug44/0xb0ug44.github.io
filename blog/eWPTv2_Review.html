I passed the eWPTv2 certification from INE and wanted to share what helped me most. This review is written for learners who already understand the basics and want a realistic path into web application pentesting.

My Background

I come from a cybersecurity and forensic computing engineering background, with practical offensive-security experience through labs, CTFs, and real-world style environments. I was not starting from zero, but I still treated eWPTv2 as a serious benchmark for web testing depth.

Course Review

The training track is long and detailed, but it is worth finishing. What stood out to me was the practical teaching style: less theory-heavy repetition, more real attack flow, tool usage, and decision-making in context. If you stay consistent, this course builds the right mindset for web assessments.

Exam Overview

  • Duration: 10 hours
  • Format: 50 questions (multiple-choice + text input)
  • Environment: Cloud machine with no internet access, preloaded with required tools and scripts
  • Difficulty: Structured and fair, but demanding under time pressure

My Strategy

exam-workflow.md method 
- Read Letter of Engagement carefully
- Enumerate each app/domain fully before answering
- Keep notes per target (auth, endpoints, vulns, creds)
- Group answers by application to save time
- Take short breaks to avoid decision fatigue

I recommend sticking to the core course tooling. You do not need exotic scripts to pass. Solid methodology, accurate notes, and careful validation are usually enough.

Key Insights From the eWPT Guide

One important takeaway is that eWPT should be treated as a practical benchmark, not a theory checkpoint. The exam expects you to combine reconnaissance, analysis, exploitation, and validation in a structured workflow. Knowing a tool name is not enough; speed and decision-making under time pressure matter a lot.

Another strong point is preparation quality. The official INE path gives a solid baseline, but hands-on repetition is what makes the difference. Keeping indexed notes for common payload patterns, fuzzing strategies, authentication attack cases, and web-service findings can save critical minutes during the exam window.

The guide also emphasizes realistic expectations: passing eWPT shows useful job-ready fundamentals, but it is still one step in a longer growth path. Continued lab practice, stronger methodology, and better reporting discipline are what convert certification success into consistent real-world web pentesting performance.
